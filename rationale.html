What was cut from the brief, and why

RepetitionPillars, tagline, "approach" and "sustainable growth" each restated 3–5 times. Each now appears once, where it does the most work.
Service sprawl~200 bullet items. The homepage shows 16; pillar pages show the rest, trimmed of near-duplicates (e.g. "Standard operating procedures" appeared in two categories).
Abstract phrasing"Institutional value creation", "organisational financial architecture", "integrated perspectives" replaced with plain descriptions of the work.
Unbacked claims"Leading academic institutions", "established consulting organisations" removed until names can be cited.
Phase 2 itemsIP & patent services shown only as a chip; launch them fully once a qualified associate is in place. Insights hidden until stocked.
Developer notesSEO, CMS, analytics and SSL requirements are build tasks, not page content — kept out of the wireframe. SSL and domain are already done.
